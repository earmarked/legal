Why Earmarked?

You start with a simple household budget. Then life happens.

A partner. Kids. A rental property (or three). An investment company. A family trust. Maybe a side hustle.

Suddenly, your finances are scattered across spreadsheets, and you’re spending your weekends learning how to use an accounting software, all just to get a clear picture of where you stand.

That’s where Earmarked comes in.

The structure you need to keep your finances organised as life gets more complicated, with simplicity, security and privacy built in.

Who are we?

We’re a team of accountants and software developers based in Melbourne, Australia. We believe managing your finances shouldn’t require a finance degree, a spreadsheet obsession, or your entire Sunday afternoon.
